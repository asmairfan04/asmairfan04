## 📄 Research & Publications

> **A Hybrid Deep Learning Framework for Alzheimer's Detection Through Voice Biomarkers**
> Z. R. Sheikh, **A. Irfan**, & N. Yazdi
> *International Conference on Speech Prosody — Accepted & Presented, May 2026*

Developed a hybrid audio classification system combining MFCCs, Mel-spectrograms, and transfer learning (YAMNet, Deep Neural Network, Random Forest) to detect Alzheimer's disease from speech biomarkers — achieving **89.5% classification accuracy**.
